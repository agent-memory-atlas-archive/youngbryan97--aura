# Native source-erasure control, 2026-09-26

The fitted native decoder has learned both an output wire and a relationship
between requests and programs. An unfitted decoder cannot distinguish those
contributions. This control fits the same native suffix without fitting-source
content tokens, then calibrates and evaluates with intact requests.

## Matched Design

The reference is
`~/.aura/rlc-evidence/semantic-native-relative-metric-single-fold0-20260926/`.
Keep its 303 fitting sources, 185 source-calibration requests, 50 held requests,
320 updates, seed, relational metric objective, witnessed contrasts, role-relative
wire, one-layer rank-8 suffix, and complete source-calibration checkpoint rule.
Only fitting-source content is replaced with repetitions of one literal token.
Template tokens, sequence length, target tokens, and supervised positions stay
unchanged. Calibration and held requests are never erased.

Length, template position, and output-prefix structure remain nuisance signals.
This removes content tokens, not every possible source-correlated shortcut.
Checkpoint selection still uses source calibration, not held labels. Neither
arm has serving or fusion authority. Compare paired outcomes and complete
source-intervention decodes before attributing a difference to semantic learning.
A positive result still would not establish broad reasoning or frontier gain.

erasure; historical version-1 evidence cannot acquire that label. Each erased
sequence records its source digest, changed positions, filler token, continuation
boundary, and unchanged supervised positions. The separate CPU verifier rebuilds
every fitting and calibration sequence from the bound source corpus and tokenizer,
including its witnessed alternative programs.
