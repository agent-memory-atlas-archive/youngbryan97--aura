## Connection

The source-grounded candidate union and scoped computation loop now have one
entry point: `run_grounded_semantic_computation_loop`. It verifies the common
source scope and retained union receipt, executes each distinct candidate in
the common input coordinates, and feeds the resulting portfolio through the
existing equation graph, observation admission, recomputation, retrieval and
recipe-retention path.

Matching programs preserve every method's provenance. They are not duplicate
votes for correctness. A requested incumbent origin must identify exactly one
candidate. The receipt records both the original incumbent and the initial
executable selection, which can differ after a real incumbent execution failure.
