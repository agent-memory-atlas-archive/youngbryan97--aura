# Native role source-control result

The FP32 native grammar scorer used exact frozen-source trie reuse on six
target-blind, three-step role-intervention requests (three paired source
changes, seed 1618033). The fitted arm completed 6/6 exact programs and
answers, and all 3/3 pairs changed the intended role binding. A matched
unfitted arm completed 2/6 exact programs and answers, with 1/3 exact pairs.
All four unfitted failures first diverged from the fitted trace at operation
choice (`at` or `count_of` versus `add`), so this comparison does not isolate
learned role binding from learned operation selection.

Removing only source-content tokens from the fitted scorer changed all six
programs: 0/6 were exact and 0/3 pairs remained source-responsive. The
independent meaning audit found a counterexample to the target meaning for
each erased program. Substituting each paired source instead produced 6/6
programs exactly matching the partner's intact-source program, with 3/3
exact swap pairs. The matched comparator reconstructed source pairs and
reverified all three arm reports before adjudication. These results establish
source-dependent graph choice on this constructed cohort, not general
transfer or broad reasoning gain.

The full-capture versus trie FP32 probe compared all 56 choices of one
training source across 12 decision competitions. All rankings agreed; the
maximum target-log-probability difference was 0.000121, below the unchanged
0.015625 per-target allowance. On the first two held role requests, all 22
decision winners matched saved full-capture traces, with maximum summed-score
difference 0.000208. The trie path has its own v7 plan and independent
source-anchor/branch-count replay. It is opt-in research execution only;
native-arithmetic equivalence and serving authority are not claimed.
